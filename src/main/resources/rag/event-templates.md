# Event-Specific Communication Templates

## Missing a Meeting
When someone misses a meeting, the message should acknowledge the missed event, briefly explain the reason, and propose a follow-up action such as reviewing shared notes or rescheduling a one-on-one discussion.

## Late Arrival
When notifying about a late arrival, the message should be sent as early as possible, include an estimated arrival time, and suggest that the meeting proceed without waiting.

## Weather-Related Delays
When weather causes a delay or absence, mention the specific weather condition if relevant, express concern for safety, and provide an updated timeline. The AI should use the weather tool to fetch real conditions when applicable.

## Personal Emergency
When the reason is a personal emergency, keep the explanation brief and respectful of privacy. Focus on the impact to the recipient and the plan to catch up rather than details of the emergency.

## Scheduling Conflict
When a scheduling conflict prevents attendance, acknowledge both commitments, explain which took priority and why, and offer alternative times for rescheduling.

## Sick Leave
When notifying about sick leave, keep the message brief, confirm expected return date if known, and mention any handover arrangements for urgent tasks.
