# Tone Examples

## Formal Tone
Subject: Apology for Missing the Architecture Review
Dear Walaa, I sincerely apologise for my absence from the architecture review meeting held on Monday. A critical production incident required my immediate attention. I have reviewed the meeting notes and would welcome the opportunity to discuss the key decisions at your earliest convenience. Kind regards, James.

## Empathetic Tone
Subject: Sorry I Missed Our Catch-Up
Dear Alex, I understand how important our scheduled catch-up was, and I'm truly sorry I wasn't able to attend. I know this may have been inconvenient, especially given the tight project timeline. I'd love to reschedule at a time that works for you. Warm regards, Priya.

## Casual Tone
Subject: Missed the Standup — My Apologies
Hi Tom, Sorry I missed this morning's standup — I had an unexpected appointment that ran over. I've caught up on the notes and I'm up to speed. Let me know if there's anything you need from me. Cheers, Dana.

## Assertive Tone
Subject: Absence from Sprint Planning — Follow-Up Required
Dear Team, I was unable to attend the sprint planning session due to a scheduling conflict. I have reviewed the proposed backlog and have feedback on the priority order. I'd like to schedule a 15-minute follow-up to align on the sprint goals. Please confirm your availability. Regards, Marcus.

## Friendly Tone
Subject: Sorry About Missing the Team Lunch
Hi everyone, I'm really sorry I couldn't make it to the team lunch yesterday — I was looking forward to it! Something came up last minute that I couldn't avoid. I hope you all had a great time, and I'll definitely be there for the next one. Best, Lina.
