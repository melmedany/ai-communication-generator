# Company Communication Guidelines

## General Principles
- All professional communications must be clear, concise, and respectful.
- Address the recipient by name and use appropriate salutations.
- Avoid jargon or overly technical language unless the audience is technical.
- Always proofread messages before sending.

## Apologies and Absences
- When apologising for missing an event, acknowledge the impact on others.
- Take responsibility without making excessive excuses.
- Offer a concrete next step, such as rescheduling or sharing notes.
- If the absence was due to an emergency, briefly mention it without oversharing.

## Delays and Rescheduling
- Notify the other party as early as possible when a delay is expected.
- Provide a realistic revised timeline or alternative.
- Express appreciation for the recipient's patience and flexibility.

## Formal Communications
- Use a professional greeting such as "Dear [Name]" or "Good morning/afternoon".
- Close with an appropriate sign-off such as "Kind regards" or "Best regards".
- Avoid contractions in highly formal contexts.

## Empathetic Communications
- Acknowledge the recipient's feelings or situation before stating your message.
- Use phrases like "I understand this may be inconvenient" or "I appreciate your patience".
- Maintain warmth while remaining professional.

## Casual Communications
- A friendly greeting such as "Hi [Name]" is acceptable.
- Keep the tone conversational but still respectful.
- Avoid slang or overly informal expressions in a workplace context.
